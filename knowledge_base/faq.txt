Company Name: AI Learning Hub

ABOUT US

AI Learning Hub is an online learning platform focused on Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, and Generative AI. We provide practical learning resources and project-based courses for students and professionals.

COURSES

We offer courses in Python, Data Science, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Generative AI, Large Language Models, and RAG.

BEGINNER COURSE

Our beginner AI course is designed for students and people who are new to Artificial Intelligence. It starts with Python and basic machine learning concepts before moving into practical AI projects.

GENERATIVE AI COURSE

Our Generative AI course covers LLMs, prompt engineering, embeddings, vector databases, RAG, LangChain, LangGraph, and AI agents.
